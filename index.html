First 90 days

Rules that protect your future

  • Never invest money you need for food, rent, medicine or bills.
  • Never borrow to invest in something you don't understand.
  • A high advertised return is not a safe investment.
  • Keep business money separate from personal spending.
  • Reinvest only when there's evidence it earns a worthwhile return.
  • Don't buy courses, gear or stock just because you feel you must spend to progress.
  • Let your lifestyle grow slower than your income.
  • Measure success by what you own, what you owe, and how reliably income covers needs.

03 · Roadmap

YEAR 1 · FOUNDATION

Safety net and skills

  • Build 3–6 months of essential expenses in a PDIC-insured account.
  • Pick one income skill to push: video editing or IT work.
  • Build a portfolio of 5–10 real samples and start freelancing.
  • Target income: ₱20,000–₱30,000/month.
YEAR 2 · IGNITE

Start investing, raise rates

  • With the emergency fund full, automate a low-cost index fund contribution.
  • Raise prices every few clients; aim for recurring clients.
  • Target savings: ₱1,500–₱2,000/week.
YEARS 3–4 · SCALE

Specialize and earn more

  • Move into higher-paying niches: motion graphics, automation, cloud, security, data.
  • Consider remote work for foreign employers, which often pays far more.
  • Keep lifestyle growth well below income growth. Target savings: ₱3,000+/week.
YEAR 5 · REVIEW

Measure and adjust

  • Compare your balance to ₱1M. If short, the same habits usually get you there in 6–7 years.
  • Move money you need soon out of stocks.
This 5-year plan is the fast track. The 10-year roadmap on Home is the full journey — use both.

05 · Rules of the system

  • Pay yourself first: automate transfers on payday.
  • Track income and expenses; review once a month.
  • Avoid high-interest debt such as credit cards and online lending apps.
  • Never keep money you need within 3–5 years in stocks.
  • Skip crypto, leverage, "signals" and doubling schemes as your foundation.

// the millionaire plan

Zero to ₱1M, ₱10M and beyond

You don't become a millionaire by looking rich. You become one by building assets that make you money. Focus on three things:

1
Increase your earning power
2
Build a business with consistent cash flow
3
Invest the profits into assets that compound
You don't need 10 businesses, a perfect idea or a rich family. You need a valuable skill, a way to sell it, disciplined money management, and enough time to let the numbers work.
